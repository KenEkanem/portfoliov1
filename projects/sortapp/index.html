Product engineering · Event operations

SortApp + CheckinApp

A modular guest operations system built to make high-profile event access simple, personal, and fast.

Overview

Event teams often manage guest information across spreadsheets, email tools, and manual check-in lists. SortApp brings those steps into one focused workflow.

The application imports guest data from CSV files, creates personalized access cards with unique QR codes, and sends them by email through @UsePlunk.

At the event

CheckinApp gives the team a quick way to search for guests and validate their access on arrival. This reduces manual coordination and keeps the entrance moving.
