Landest Infrastructure

A dependable cloud foundation for a growing property technology platform.

Overview

Landest needed an infrastructure foundation that could support its core services, external integrations, and continued product growth without making operations fragile.

I provisioned and deployed the API, gateway, and Zenith Bank proxy services on AWS, creating a clear route from external requests to internal services.

Operations and visibility

I configured a Docker-based Mailcow server and introduced OpenTelemetry across operations, bringing traces, metrics, and logs into one observability backend.
